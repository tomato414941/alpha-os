## Caveat

Long-vol tickets use a simple ATM straddle proxy from public Deribit summary quotes. They still lack order-book depth, delta hedge PnL, margin, assignment/expiry handling, and realized-vol forecasts. Short premium candidates must be treated as capped-risk structures, not naked short options.
