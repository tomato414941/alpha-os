# Token Unlocks Results

This lane was added to cover scheduled supply events as a non-price,
event-driven profit-source candidate.

Initial result:

- CryptoBeacon exposes current 30-day cliff unlocks in static page text.
- The first useful output is a tradable-market join, not a standalone short.
- Large unlocks such as HYPE, ZRO, H, ENA, SUI, OPN, and ZORA should be labeled
  around their event windows before any promotion.

Current paper tickets:

  and positive funding means the short side receives carry.
- ZRO: short paper candidate. Higher percent-supply unlock and positive
  funding align, but venue liquidity is much thinner than HYPE.
- KAITO and EIGEN are additional short paper candidates, but visible liquidity
  and impact are weaker.
- ME is not a clean short candidate despite the large supply shock. Negative
  funding means new shorts may be crowded, so it is tracked as crowded-short
  squeeze risk instead.
- These are event-window paper tickets, not live trade instructions. They need
  return labels around the unlock window, funding persistence, depth decay,
  and stop logic.
